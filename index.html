The Ember & Oak feeling

Fire brings us
back to the good stuff.

We believe the best meals don't need much fuss. Just beautiful ingredients, a live fire, good people, and time enough to enjoy all three.

Our kitchen follows the rhythm of the seasons. We work with thoughtful growers, cook with patience, and let the wood do what it does best: bring depth, warmth and a little theatre to the table.
