Why we light the fire

E&O

We believe the best things are rarely complicated.

Cooking over fire asks us to slow down, pay attention, and let good ingredients speak for themselves.

At Ember & Oak, the flame is more than a cooking method. It is the centre of the room, the reason to gather, and a reminder that a meal can be both beautifully simple and worth remembering. We make food with care, welcome people without fuss, and hope you leave feeling a little more connected than when you arrived.

A little history

From a spark
to a gathering place.

Our story is a simple one, built around a shared love of open-fire cooking and the feeling of a full table.

  1. The idea

    A meal worth slowing down for

    Over a long dinner, a small group of food lovers imagined a neighbourhood restaurant where the hearth—not the clock—sets the rhythm.

  2. The first flame

    A kitchen built around fire

    They found a home for the idea, shaped a kitchen around live embers, and began building relationships with growers and makers.

  3. Today

    Always a seat to share

    Now the menu changes with the market, the team keeps learning, and the welcome stays the same: come as you are, and bring an appetite.

A fictional story created for this portfolio project.

Good things, close to the source

Follow the seasons.
Know the hands.

Our best dishes begin long before they reach the kitchen.

We aim to work with growers, ranchers, fishers, and makers who care about the land and the quality of what they produce. The menu follows what is at its best, rather than asking the season to follow us.

  • 01
    Season-led produce

    Vegetables and fruit chosen for flavour, freshness, and their moment in the year.

  • 02
    Thoughtful proteins

    Menus planned around responsible choices and careful use of each ingredient.

  • 03
    Less waste, more care

    Stocks, pickles, sauces, and daily specials help us make good use of what we bring in.

Our sourcing principles are part of this fictional restaurant concept; no supplier partnerships are claimed.
