Ask us a question ↗
Can you accommodate dietary requirements?

We’re happy to help with allergies and dietary needs. Our kitchen handles common allergens, so we cannot guarantee any dish is free of them. Please tell your server about any allergy before ordering.

Do you accept walk-ins?

We welcome walk-ins whenever space allows. For a particular time or a larger group, we recommend requesting a table ahead of your visit.

Can I book for a group?

For seven or more guests, please contact the team directly. We’ll talk through the occasion and help find the right arrangement.

Is there parking nearby?

There are public parking options around the Old Town neighbourhood. Allow a little extra time during busy evening periods.

Are children welcome?

Of course. We’re glad to welcome guests of all ages. Please let us know if you need space for a high chair.
